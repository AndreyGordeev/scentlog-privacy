ScentLog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

ScentLog is a training journal for dog scent-work handlers. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with your data. It is deliberately short because ScentLog does very little with your data: it keeps your journal on your own device and nowhere else.

The three facts below are the whole of it, and they match word-for-word what the app shows you on its Privacy screen (More → Privacy → What we store):

  • Your journal stays on this device.
  • It's included in your Google account backup if backup is on.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We don't collect analytics.

What ScentLog stores, and where

Everything you enter — your dogs, your training runs, your trial records and titles — is stored in a database on your device. ScentLog has no account, no login and no server. We, the developer, cannot see your journal, because it never leaves your phone by any path we control.

The only ways a copy of your data can leave the device are ones you turn on or trigger yourself:

  • Google Auto Backup. If Android backup is enabled for your Google account, Android may include ScentLog's journal in your personal device backup, under your Google account. This is a platform feature between you and Google; the developer has no access to it. If a backup exists, it is what restores your journal when you set up a new phone. Your unlocks are not included in this backup — a new phone re-checks them with Google Play.
  • Manual export. ScentLog lets you export your journal to a file you choose (More → Data → Export). That file is yours; where it goes afterwards is up to you.
  • PDF report sharing. When you create a report and share it, you choose the recipient through Android's share sheet.

Payments

ScentLog is free to log. Optional unlocks are one-time purchases, not a subscription, and they are processed by Google Play Billing. ScentLog never sees or stores your payment details — Google Play handles the entire transaction. The app only keeps a local record of which unlocks you own, and that record stays on the device (it is excluded from backup, so a restored or new device re-checks what you own with Google Play).

Analytics, ads and trackers — none

ScentLog contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising and no third-party trackers of any kind. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only network access in the build is Google Play Billing's, used solely to process and verify purchases. There is nothing that profiles you, follows you across apps, or reports your behaviour to us or anyone else.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of your data, and you can erase it at any time:

  1. Open More → Privacy → Delete all data.
  2. Type DELETE to confirm, then choose Delete everything.

This permanently removes all of your ScentLog data from the device — every dog, run, trial and title, together with the app's settings, your cached purchase status, any dog photos and any generated report files. Because ScentLog keeps no server-side copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no separate deletion request to send: erasing it on your device erases it completely.

The one caveat is Google Auto Backup: if you had backup turned on, a copy may remain in your own Google account backup until Google refreshes or you remove it, exactly as with any other app's backup. That copy lives in your Google account, not with the developer. If you would like a portable copy before you delete, use More → Data → Export first.

For instructors — student data on your device

If you buy the Instructor Pack, students can send you their journals as report packages. This section explains, in plain language, what that means for the data involved.

  • What third-party data reaches your device. When you accept a student's report package, their data is placed on your device: the student's name, their dog's call name, and that dog's training journal (its runs, trials and title progress). Nothing else about the student is collected.
  • It is stored locally, indefinitely, until you delete it. Received student data lives only on your device and stays there for as long as you keep it — there is no expiry — until you remove it.
  • Backup covers your roster, not the received journals. Your student roster (the students and dogs you have added) is included in your own Google account backup; the received student journals themselves are not included in backup, so a new device starts without them until the packages are accepted again.
  • Deleting a student removes all of their data in one action. Removing a student deletes everything of theirs from your device at once — their roster entry and every received journal — with a single action.
  • There is no instructor→student channel. ScentLog provides no back-channel from instructor to student: reading a student's journal sends nothing back, and the app cannot push anything to a student. The flow is one-way, student to instructor, and only when the student chooses to send.

Children

ScentLog is a tool for dog handlers and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information for anyone.
